Privacy

Last updated 2 October 2026

This website is a simple, static site. It sets no cookies, runs no analytics and uses no tracking scripts.

What we collect

Nothing automatically. The only personal data we receive is what you choose to send by email to hello@cambyte.co.uk. We use it to reply to you and, if you become a client, to do the work you ask for. We do not sell it or share it with anyone else, except service providers we need in order to deliver the work.

Hosting

The site is served by Cloudflare, which processes standard connection data (such as IP address) to deliver pages and protect against abuse. See Cloudflare's privacy policy. Fonts are loaded from Google Fonts, which may log your IP address when the font file is requested.

Links

Links to the App Store, GitHub, LinkedIn, TikTok and YouTube take you to those services, which have their own privacy policies.

Your rights

Under UK GDPR you can ask what data we hold about you, ask for it to be corrected or deleted, or object to its use. Email hello@cambyte.co.uk.

Who we are

Cambyte is run by Roberto Pires Almeida, Cambridge, UK.
