Privacy

Last updated 28 October 2026

This website is a simple, static site. It sets no cookies, runs no analytics and uses no tracking scripts. Stripe's checkout page, which you only reach if you choose to pay, is covered below.

What we collect

Nothing automatically. We receive personal data only when you email us at hello@cambyte.co.uk or order and pay online. We use it to reply to you and, if you become a client, to do the work you ask for and keep business records. We do not sell it or share it with anyone else, except the service providers below that we need in order to deliver the work.

Email is handled by Google Workspace. See Google's privacy policy. We keep emails for as long as we need them to reply to you or, for clients, for as long as the law requires us to keep business records.

Website assistant

The assistant on our home page runs in your browser and answers from the information on that page. Nothing you type into it is sent to us or to anyone else. If you use its microphone button, your browser’s own speech service turns your voice into text: Google for Chrome, Apple for Safari, or your browser maker for others. Their privacy policy covers that step, not ours, and we never receive the recording. Read-aloud mostly plays short clips made in advance with an AI voice and loaded from this website, the same way a picture loads, so nothing you say or type is sent to make them. Any other reply is read by your browser’s own voice.

Paying online

When you choose to pay, the “Pay” button takes you to a checkout page run by Stripe. Stripe collects your name, email address, card details and the business or project name you type in, and passes us everything except your full card details: we never see or store your card number. The link also carries a short order code that lists your choices (for example the package and extras), so we can match the payment to your order. It contains no personal data.

Stripe uses the data to process the payment, prevent fraud and meet its legal duties, and sets its own cookies on its checkout page. See Stripe's privacy policy. We keep payment records for as long as UK law requires us to keep business records.

Hosting

The site is hosted on GitHub Pages, run by GitHub, Inc., which processes standard connection data (such as IP address) to deliver pages and protect against abuse. See GitHub's privacy statement. Fonts are loaded from Google Fonts, which may log your IP address when the font file is requested.

Links

Links to the App Store, GitHub, LinkedIn, TikTok and YouTube take you to those services, which have their own privacy policies.

Your rights

Under UK GDPR you can ask what data we hold about you, ask for it to be corrected or deleted, or object to its use. Email hello@cambyte.co.uk. If you are unhappy with how we handle your data, you can complain to the Information Commissioner's Office.

Who we are
