Questions

Things people ask before they email.

Who owns the app and the code?

You do, fully, once the final payment clears. Source code, design files, the App Store listing and any accounts are handed over in your name. If you ever want another developer to take it on, they can.

Do I need an Apple developer account?

Yes. Apple requires apps to be published under the account of the business that owns them, so I set one up with you on day one. It costs £79 a year, paid to Apple. Approval from Apple can take a few days for a sole trader and up to two weeks for a limited company, so we start that first while I build.

How long does Apple's review take?

Usually one to three days, occasionally a week. I prepare the submission to pass first time and handle any questions from Apple myself. I never promise a public launch date, because that last step belongs to Apple.

What about Android?

Every app can have an Android version, built natively in Kotlin, for 40% on top of the iPhone price. Most small businesses in the UK start with iPhone and add Android once the app is earning its keep.

Why are you so much cheaper than an agency?

One person does the design, the code, the testing and the release. There is no office, no project manager and no outsourcing to pay for. I also use modern AI tooling and reusable, tested foundations, so a Starter app takes days of real work rather than months of billable ones. The quality bar is the app you can download above.

What if I want changes after launch?

On the one-off option, email me and you get a quote, usually in hours at £45 an hour. On a care plan you have a bank of hours each month and just ask. Either way, nothing is done without you agreeing it first.

Can you fix an app someone else built?

Usually, yes. Send me what you have and I will take a free look. Rescue work is quoted as a fixed fee, and a port to a modern codebase is often cheaper than you fear.

Do you work outside Cambridge?

Yes. Everything can be done by video call and TestFlight, so location does not matter. Cambridge businesses get the coffee option.
