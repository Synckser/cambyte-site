    How it works

    No jargon. No surprises on the invoice.

    1. Ask, then email

      My AI assistant on this page answers questions about apps, prices and ordering at any hour. When you are ready, email me about the business and I ask the awkward questions. If a call would help, I arrange one by phone or video, from any country. No obligation.

    2. Written plan

      Within two days you get a one-page plan: what the app does, screen by screen, the price and the date. Starter and Business keep their website price; Custom gets a fixed quote. Agree it and we start within a week (buying as a private person: after your 14-day cancellation period, unless you ask to start sooner).

    3. Build, with you watching

      A test version on your own phone within the first week, then updates every few days. You see it grow and steer it.

    4. Launch and handover

      I submit to Apple and deal with their review, which usually takes one to three days (the launch date is Apple's call). Then you get the code, the accounts and a plain-English guide. After that, care plan or goodbye, your call.

    Questions

    Things people ask before they email.

    How does paying online work?

    For a Starter or Business app, choose your extras in the order form under the prices and pay the 50% deposit on Stripe's secure checkout. I then email you, usually the same working day, to book a kickoff call and agree the written plan. Buying as a private person, the work waits until your 14-day cancellation period ends, unless you tick the box to start sooner. The other 50% is invoiced when Apple approves your app, and a care plan, if you chose one, starts the month after launch. If what you need turns out bigger than the package, I tell you in writing before doing any extra work, and if you would rather not go ahead you get the whole deposit back. The details are in the terms.

    Who owns the app and the code?

    You do, fully, once the final payment clears. Source code, design files, the App Store listing and any accounts are handed over in your name. If you ever want another developer to take it on, they can.

    Do I need an Apple developer account?

    Yes. Apple requires apps to be published under the account of the business that owns them, so I set one up with you on day one. It costs £79 a year, paid to Apple. Approval from Apple can take a few days for a sole trader and up to two weeks for a limited company, so we start that first while I build. Apple's review then usually takes one to three days. I handle it, but I never promise a public launch date, because that last step belongs to Apple.

    What about Android?

    Every app can have an Android version, built natively in Kotlin, for 40% on top of the iPhone price. Many small businesses start with iPhone and add Android once the app is earning its keep. If most of your customers use Android, say so when you email and we plan for both from the start.

    What if I want changes after launch?

    Without a care plan, email me and you get a quote, usually in hours at £45 an hour. On a care plan you have a bank of hours each month and just ask. Either way, nothing is done without you agreeing it first.

    Do you take projects from outside the UK?

    Yes, from any country. Everything is done by email, test builds on your own phone and, when needed, a video call, so location does not matter. I work in English or Portuguese and arrange any call at a time that suits your time zone. Businesses near Cambridge can also meet for coffee.

    How much does AI work cost?

    There is no price list, because every business is different. Email me how your business runs and you get a fixed written quote, and nothing starts until you agree it.
